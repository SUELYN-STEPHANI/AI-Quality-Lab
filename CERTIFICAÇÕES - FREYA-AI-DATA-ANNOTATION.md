# Freya — AI Data Annotation & Live Transcription

## Training Overview

Specialized training in audio annotation, conversational transcription and
quality control for datasets used in Artificial Intelligence training.

The program focuses on transforming natural human speech into structured,
consistent and high-quality annotated data while following strict project
guidelines.

Unlike conventional transcription, the workflow requires the evaluator to
analyze the complete structure of a conversation, including speaker changes,
interruptions, pauses, overlapping speech, audio quality and segmentation.

---

## Core Competencies Developed

### 1. AI Data Annotation

Understanding the role of human annotation in the creation of datasets used
to train and evaluate speech and language AI systems.

Competencies include:

- Dataset quality awareness
- Annotation consistency
- Guideline interpretation
- Structured labeling
- Error prevention
- Quality control
- Final task validation

---

### 2. Audio Structure Analysis

Before transcription begins, the audio must be analyzed as a structured
conversation.

This includes identifying:

- Number of speakers
- Speaker changes
- Interruptions
- Pauses
- Overlapping speech
- Background noise
- Audio quality limitations
- Conversation structure

The objective is to understand the audio before creating annotations.

---

### 3. Speech Segmentation

Training in dividing continuous audio into accurate speech segments.

Evaluation includes:

- Beginning of speech
- End of speech
- Speaker changes
- Silence and pauses
- Continuous speech
- Segment boundaries
- Timestamp accuracy
- Overlapping speakers

Segmentation quality is critical because an accurate transcription can still
be considered incorrect when associated with an incorrectly defined segment.

---

### 4. Speaker Identification & Speaker Turns

Training in maintaining consistent speaker identification throughout a
conversation.

Skills developed:

- Speaker differentiation
- Speaker labeling
- Speaker turn consistency
- Speaker change detection
- Overlapping speaker handling
- Avoiding speaker attribution errors

---

### 5. Verbatim Transcription

Application of strict verbatim transcription principles.

The objective is to represent what was actually said rather than rewriting
speech into grammatically improved language.

The evaluator must preserve:

- Natural speech patterns
- Repetitions
- False starts
- Incomplete words
- Grammatical mistakes made by speakers
- Conversational structures

The fundamental principle is:

> Transcribe what was said — do not rewrite what the speaker intended to say.

---

### 6. Annotation Tags

Training in the correct application of annotation tags for situations that
require additional information.

This involves recognizing when special markup is required and distinguishing
between normal conversational phenomena and events that require annotation.

---

### 7. Audio Quality Assessment

Evaluation of whether recording conditions affect annotation reliability.

Factors considered include:

- Background noise
- Difficult accents
- Poor recording quality
- Unintelligible speech
- Synthetic voices
- Language differences
- Overlapping speech

---

### 8. Quality Assurance

Every completed annotation must undergo final quality review.

The review includes:

- Timestamps
- Segmentation
- Speaker identification
- Labels
- Tags
- Audio quality classification
- Transcription accuracy

The task is considered complete only after this validation.

---

## Official Annotation Workflow

The methodology follows a structured seven-step process:

1. Review the Audio
2. Annotate and Label Speech
3. Apply Speaker/Gender Labels
4. Transcribe Speech
5. Apply Tags
6. Evaluate Audio Quality
7. Review and Submit

This workflow is designed to reduce annotation errors and improve consistency
between different evaluators.

---

## Quality Principles

The training emphasizes four central principles:

**Accuracy**

Annotations must faithfully represent the source audio.

**Consistency**

The same rules must be applied across all tasks.

**Guideline Compliance**

Personal interpretation must not replace project rules.

**Review**

Every task must be validated before submission.
