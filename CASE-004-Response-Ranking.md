# Objective

Evaluate multiple AI-generated responses to the same prompt and determine the preferred answer based on a standardized quality framework.

This study demonstrates the ability to compare competing responses, justify ranking decisions, and identify quality differences beyond factual correctness.

---

# Prompt

Explain climate change in no more than 100 words for a high school student.

---

# Candidate Responses

## Response A

Climate change is the long-term change in Earth's temperatures and weather patterns, mainly caused by human activities such as burning fossil fuels. These activities release greenhouse gases that trap heat in the atmosphere. The effects include rising sea levels, stronger storms and more frequent heatwaves.

---

## Response B

Climate change happens because pollution warms the planet. The weather becomes hotter and nature suffers. People should recycle and drive less to help solve the problem.

---

## Response C

Climate change refers to significant long-term shifts in global temperatures and weather patterns. Human activities, particularly greenhouse gas emissions from fossil fuels, are the primary cause. The consequences include rising sea levels, biodiversity loss and more frequent extreme weather events. Reducing emissions and adopting sustainable practices can help mitigate these effects.

---

# Evaluation

| Response | Score |
|----------|------:|
| Response C | 5 |
| Response A | 4 |
| Response B | 2 |

---

# Ranking

🥇 Response C

🥈 Response A

🥉 Response B

---

# Justification

Response C provides the most complete explanation while remaining within the requested length. It accurately identifies the primary causes of climate change, explains the consequences, and briefly introduces mitigation strategies without unnecessary detail.

Response A is technically accurate and easy to understand but omits possible solutions, making the answer slightly less complete.

Response B is understandable but oversimplifies the topic, lacks important scientific context, and does not fully address the user's request.

---

# Conclusion

Response ranking requires more than identifying factual correctness. It involves assessing completeness, instruction adherence, clarity, usefulness and overall communication quality.

This study demonstrates a structured approach to comparative evaluation and preference ranking, a common task in AI Quality workflows.
